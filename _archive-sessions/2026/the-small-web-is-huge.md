Come for an exploration of the "small" web. We'll explore what the small web is, why it's blowing up, how to participate in it as a writer and a user, and how to have FUN surfing the web again.

There are a handful of huge websites that dominate the Internet and control the vast majority of users. This practice is antithetical to a free and open world wide web. In open rebellion to this trend, many Internet users have migrated some or all of their digital footprints onto smaller group platforms or individual sites.

This talk will explore as many facets of this phenomenon as can fit in the timeslot.
