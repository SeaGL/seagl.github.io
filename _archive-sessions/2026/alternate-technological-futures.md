This talk will discuss the importance of environmentally-minded technologists, how you can become one, & what technologies there are for us to be excited for. Additionally it will discuss why "AI" isn't amongst them.

It was originally presented at the Wakaatu Arts & Action! Club in Nelson, New Zealand.
