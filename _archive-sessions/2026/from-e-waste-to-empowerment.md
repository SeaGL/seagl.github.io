A small effort to keep old laptops out of landfills grew into The Computer Upcycle Project, a community upcycling program that's refurbished 3,000+ computers for people in need. This talk shares how a local donation program in Olympia, WA turned into a full-scale computer reuse effort — breathing new life into old hardware with a mix of Linux systems, including Debian, Bazzite, Fedora, and our own Nixbook OS, a simple, stable, auto-updating NixOS-based build. Learn how thoughtful design, the right OS for the right machine, and community action turn e-waste into opportunity.
