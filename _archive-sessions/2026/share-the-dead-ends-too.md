Open hardware and open software run on shared knowledge, but we mostly share the things that worked. The dead ends and half-broken attempts stay private, even though they often save the next person the most time. I want to make the case for sharing those too. My example is a flashlight I tried to 3D print from filament that conducts electricity. The first version barely lit up, and I posted it anyway as a "limited success." The second one worked. I will walk through both, with real measurements and a straight answer on what the filament is good and bad at. Cheap, printable, open hardware makes it low-risk to share the messy parts. And yes, I will bring flashlights to hand out.
